Amtgard Combat in 60 Seconds

The rules are simple enough to begin now.

Amtgard's combat system rewards control, movement, accuracy, defense, and honor. These are the core rules a newcomer needs first.

TorsoValid hit = death.
Arm or LegValid hit = that limb is wounded.
Two Limb WoundsAny valid combination = death.
Head & NeckNot legal targets. Never intentionally strike them.

When wounded

Arm: put the wounded arm behind your back.

Leg: put that knee on the ground; no hopping on the good leg.

Never part of the fight

No punching, kicking, bashing, or grappling.

The rule beneath every rule

Fight with control. Take your shots. Respect the people around you.

Your First Day

RinCon was the tutorial.

If you enjoyed the booth, the next step is wonderfully simple.

  1. Find Obsidian Gate.We play in Tucson. Check the group before traveling for current meeting details.
  2. Wear something comfortable.You do not need medieval garb for your first visit. Bring water and clothes you can move in.
  3. Say: “Hi, I'm new.”We'll connect you with the people who can help you get started.
  4. Complete the required waiver.A membership form/waiver is required before taking the field; minors require the appropriate parent or legal guardian involvement.
  5. Borrow equipment.You do not need to own a sword before you come out. Ask about loaner gear.
  6. Learn. Fight. Return.Start with the basics, try a ditch or battlegame when ready, then keep coming back.
